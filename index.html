ABOUT INAMIGOS

Turning compassion
into action.

Founded on September 23, 2020, InAmigos Foundation is a Section 8 registered non-profit organization based in Chhattisgarh.

The foundation works with volunteers, professionals and partners to address community needs through education, community support, women empowerment, animal welfare, environmental sustainability and skill development.

Its initiatives combine immediate support with opportunities designed to create meaningful and lasting social impact.

OUR INITIATIVES

Six projects.
One purpose.

Each initiative focuses on a different social or environmental need while contributing to the larger vision of positive community change.

01

COMMUNITY SUPPORT

Project Seva

Providing food and clothing support to underprivileged communities and helping address essential needs.

02

EDUCATION

Project Bachpanshala

Supporting quality education and learning opportunities for children from underprivileged communities.

03

ANIMAL WELFARE

Project Jeev

Supporting animals through feeding, protection, rescue and care while promoting compassion for every living being.

04

WOMEN EMPOWERMENT

Project Udaan

Empowering women through skills, awareness and opportunities that can support greater independence.

05

ENVIRONMENT

Project Prakriti

Promoting environmental conservation and sustainable practices for a healthier future.

06

SKILL DEVELOPMENT

Project Vikas

Developing skills and employability opportunities to help young people prepare for professional futures.
